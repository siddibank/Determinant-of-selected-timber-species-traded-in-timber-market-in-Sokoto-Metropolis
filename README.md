# Determinants of Selected Timber Species Traded in Timber Markets in Sokoto Metropolis

This repository contains research work analyzing the key economic, market, and product-quality factors influencing the selection of timber species traded in Sokoto Metropolis, Nigeria.

---

## 📌 Overview
The study examines how timber traders choose species based on price, durability, market demand, availability, and transportation cost.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Describe the socio-economic characteristics of timber trades in the study area.
- Identify the major timber species traded in Sokoto Metropolis and their source.
- Examine the factor influencing consumers  choice of timber species.
- Analyze the marketing structure of the timbertrade in the study area.
- Identify the challenges faced by the timber traders in Sokoto Metropolis.

---

## 🧪 Methodology
- **Study Area:** Sokoto Metropolis  
- **Data Collection:** Structured questionnaires administered to traders  
- **Analysis Tools:** Descriptive statistics, regression, correlation and financial profitability
- **Sampling Method:** Sampling Procedure and techniques 

---

## 🪵 Major Timber Species Studied
- Mahogany  
- Iroko
- Ofram
- Teak  


---

## 🔍 Key Determinants Identified
- **Price:** Lower prices attract more buyers and traders.  
- **Durability & Quality:** Hardwoods with long lifespan are preferred.  
- **Availability:** Easily sourced species are traded more.  
- **Market Demand:** Driven mainly by construction and furniture sectors.  
- **Transportation Cost:** Species with affordable logistics dominate trade.  
- **Trader Experience:** Influences species choice and price strategy.
